## Purpose and claim boundary

This is the held-out/profile-confirmatory continuation of the fixed-backend
frontend comparison.  It tests whether the frozen
`lineage_early_seed_churn_guard_v3` XFeat frontend preserves KLT runability and
improves agreement with the available proxy trajectory, and whether it
outperforms the tested SP+LG seed-chain frontend under the same
`VINS-Fusion-origin` backend.

The four development windows used to design v3 (`a06_s000_d045`,
`a06_s045_d045`, `a09_6000_6800`, and `h07_s000_d050`) are excluded from the
confirmatory denominator.  Results from this experiment may support only the
tested SP+LG implementation and these underwater data domains.  They may not
be described as a comparison against all learned frontends, full SuperVINS, or
HFNet-SLAM.  COLMAP and ReAqROVIO references are proxy trajectories, not
independent ground truth.

`c13ffcebe54673c4acbaa0f5db6e807f54104437339d7ac2ad12076f28549010`).

The confirmatory roster is generated by this fixed rule:

1. Retain 45 s windows with `history_excluded=false`,
   `reference_support_pass=true`, `reference_full_coverage=1.0`, and
   `selected_final=false`.  The last condition prevents reuse of the 20 P07
   learned/VINS evaluation windows.
2. Extend the history exclusion through the current freeze date using exact
   window IDs and dataset/index interval searches.  `A02:0007` is excluded
   because it overlaps the later result-informed HFNet interval
   `a02_4500_7200`; this is a provenance exclusion, not an AQUA-FE outcome
   exclusion.
3. Within each remaining sequence choose the greatest frozen degradation
   `score`; ties use the earliest start.  Retain one window per sequence so the
   scientific unit is both a window and a sequence.
4. Keep every selected sequence.  Do not remove a selected window after seeing
   frontend activation, runability, APE, RPE, or scale.  All failures remain in
   the ledger.

The resulting roster in `candidate_windows.csv` contains 12 non-overlapping
scientific units from 12 sequences and three data domains: two AFRL, seven
AQUALOC archaeology, and three AQUALOC harbor.  Using the already frozen
`tau_normal=0.10`, six are above-normal challenge windows and six are normal
no-harm windows.  This stratum is descriptive and never changes inclusion.

## Arms

The three primary arms are frozen in `arms.csv`:

1. `klt`: pure KLT/GFTT baseline.
2. `splg`: `hybrid_superpoint_lightglue` with the established early seed-chain
   profile.  This is an SP+LG frontend inside the common exporter and frozen
   VINS backend, not the full SuperVINS system.
3. `xfeat_v3`: `hybrid_xfeat` with
   `lineage_early_seed_churn_guard_v3`, including the fixed first-eligible-frame
   GFTT-birth reserve boundary `0.10`.

No LoFTR arm will be added after outcomes are visible.  It may be evaluated in
a separately preregistered secondary experiment only.

## Frozen frontend contract

Across arms and repeats, except for `method`, learned source, and the named
frontend profile:

- `MEASUREMENT_SELECTION=0`
- `EXPORT_MAX_FEATURES=350` and `VINS_MAX_CNT=350`
- `VINS_SAFE_SOURCE_SELECTION=0`
- `FORMAL_THREE_LAYER_EXPORT=0`
- `PREPROCESS=adaptive_clahe`
- `PROCESS_SKIPPED_FRAMES=1`
- `SEMIDENSE_FALLBACK_METHOD=none`
- `FORCE_EXPORT=1`, `RUN_VINS=0` during frontend generation
- `every_n=2`
- AQUALOC `FRAME_OFFSET=1`; AFRL `FRAME_OFFSET=0`, as fixed by its timestamp
  adapter
- backend quality mapping `vins_safe`, floor `0.80`, alpha `0.65`, and all
  source scales `1.0`

The final PointCloud actually received by VINS must contain at most 350 points
on every frame.  Nominal configuration alone is insufficient.  Over-budget,
missing-message, timestamp, topic-integrity, or source-identity violations are
reported as frontend integrity failures and are not silently repaired after
results.

Byte-identical all-three bags remain in the runability denominator but are
excluded from frontend accuracy attribution.  Pairwise byte-identical bags are
excluded only from the affected pairwise contrast.

## Runability and common-support gates

These values are unchanged from the earlier same-backend protocol.

- Repeat PASS: VINS initializes, produces a non-empty trajectory, and reaches
  at least `70%` output coverage.
- Window/arm PASS: all `3/3` repeats PASS.
- Accuracy eligibility: all three arms PASS all repeats, followed by an exact
  all-nine-trajectory common intersection containing at least `30` poses,
  spanning at least `10 s`, with at least `70%` common coverage and at least
  `10` strict `1 s` RPE pairs.
- Accuracy is computed only on the common poses and common 1 s RPE grid.
- Every failed or ineligible window remains visible in `runability.csv` with a
  reason; it never enters the accuracy denominator.

## Metrics and inference

Primary ordering is runability first, accuracy second.

- Primary accuracy metric: APE RMSE after each trajectory receives its own
  fixed-scale proper SE(3) alignment.
- Secondary accuracy metric: 1 s translation RPE RMSE on the common grid.
- Sim(3) APE/RPE and fitted scale are diagnostic and are always labelled; they
  never replace fixed-scale results or rescue a failed gate.
- Each arm is summarized per window by the median and full range of three
  repeats.  Window/sequence is the independent unit (`n<=12`), never replay.
- Pairwise effect is the within-window log ratio and percent change for
  `xfeat_v3/klt` and `xfeat_v3/splg`, with wins/ties/losses, median ratio, and a
  95% bootstrap confidence interval across windows.
- Confirmatory direction uses an exact two-sided sign test on the primary APE
  contrast, excluding exact ties.  The two primary comparator p-values are
  Holm-corrected.  RPE is corroborative and reported without being substituted
  for a failed APE claim.

An observed no-harm statement against KLT additionally requires that v3 add no
runability failure on any KLT-PASS window.  A closed v3 branch must be
byte-identical to KLT to receive the stronger mechanistic no-op label.  No
finite result is called a universal no-harm theorem.
