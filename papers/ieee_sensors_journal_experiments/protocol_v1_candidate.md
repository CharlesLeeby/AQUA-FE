# Protocol V1 Candidate: Selector-Independent Learned-Seeded Frontend

Protocol identity: `isj-core-method-candidate-v1`  
Status: `CANDIDATE_P03` (not a confirmatory protocol)  
Data route: `MULTI_SEQUENCE_CANDIDATE`  
Owner: IEEE Sensors Journal experiment governance bundle

This document is the P03 candidate contract. It may be promoted to
`protocol_v1.md` only after P03A/P03B shadow, calibration, canonical export,
and the P06 contract probes pass. No number in this document is a confirmatory
APE/RPE result.

## 1. Claim Boundary

The method claim is selective learned-seeded KLT measurement formation for
low-texture underwater imagery. The proposed contribution is the combination
of a KLT carrier, multi-frame probation, base-only F/H correctness eligibility,
conservative temporal reliability, and a marginal geometric-support admission
policy. The 3x3 matrix is a view-space conditioning surrogate; it is not a
6-DoF Fisher information matrix and does not establish pose observability.

The following distinctions are frozen:

- B0 is the native VINS-Fusion frontend reference.
- B1 is strong KLT/GFTT with adaptive CLAHE and the same exporter.
- B2 is an ungated learned-seed candidate and is not the proposed method.
- H is a frozen distance/grid heuristic over the same candidate stream.
- P is the calibrated marginal-support selector over that stream.
- D is an exact whole-lineage drop derived from a stable P bag.
- C-QG is an independent classical GFTT pool passed through the same QG
  selector and a trajectory-outcome-blind matched-dose audit. It is not a
  randomized, unconditional, or causal control, and it is never derived from
  the P measurement bag.
- M is a controlled same-backend modern learned frontend reference.

Existing cached learned/VINS windows remain development-only under P02 history
exclusion. The current data identity supports a multi-sequence route, not an
external-held-out cross-domain claim.

## 2. Arm and Fairness Contract

Controlled external-feature arms B1/B2/H/P/D/C-QG/M share raw image/IMU/GT,
window, preprocessing, frame stride, KLT carrier, initialization, maximum
feature dose, VINS configuration, serial replay schedule, CPU settings,
evaluator, common-support mask, and backend `q=1`. B0 retains its native
quality behavior. The backend q setting is not the selector reliability score.

H and P share the complete selector-independent master producer, including
KLT base tracks, trigger, proposal carrier, probation, survival, motion gate,
base-only F/H fit, thresholds, termination, stable IDs, B_active, total cap,
and exporter. Their only intended difference is ranking/stopping policy. A
master-pool hash mismatch after the first selection divergence invalidates the
H-P selector-only contrast.

C-QG shares the pooled reliability schema, correctness function, QG selector,
maximum caps, exporter, and evaluator, but its proposal pool is generated by
the independent frozen GFTT adapter at the same trigger events. Matching occurs
after the complete frontend log is written and before any VINS trajectory
metric is read. Rejected classical candidates cannot be promoted or rescued by
matching.

## 3. Master Candidate Stream

The producer emits `isj-master-candidate-stream-v1` JSONL. At every frame it
records:

- immutable `K_t^0`: sorted native KLT IDs, coordinates, temporal evidence,
  and a deterministic base-export subset;
- `live_learned` and `live_classical`: all not-yet-terminated candidate
  lineages, independent of arm admission;
- `eligible_learned` and `eligible_classical`: the current subset that has
  passed survival, motion, and F/H correctness eligibility;
- image width/height, trigger reason, frame/timestamp, config hash;
- base-only F/H model-fit hash, input IDs, seed, thresholds, and arbitration;
- predecessor `master_pool_hash`, plus separate learned and classical pool
  hashes.

The producer never reads H/P decisions. `live_*` prevents a transient motion
or geometry gate failure from being mistaken for termination. An ID may not
reappear after it leaves `live_*`; a stable allocator forbids ID reuse.

An arm constructs its own `L_t` by carrying previously admitted active
lineages. Its `arm_chain_hash_t` includes the predecessor arm hash, master
hash, K0/L/E track evidence, normalized z coordinates, q/e, image shape,
active slots, ordered decisions/gains/reasons, selector/model/config hashes,
backend q, and exported IDs. Accepted IDs are never re-ranked, evicted for a
new candidate, or readmitted; only the shared termination contract removes
them.

## 4. Reliability Score q_lower

For a track-lineage x decision-time row, `Y_(i,t)^h = 1` means the lineage is
KLT-valid and geometry-correct on every one of the next frozen `h=5` frames.
Rows are omitted when the sequence tail cannot supply the complete horizon.
The split is sequence-disjoint and all rows of one lineage stay in one split.

The pooled base model uses exactly these source-neutral features:

1. `age_norm_30`;
2. `ncc_score`;
3. `fb_score_tau_1px = exp(-FB_error)`;
4. `survival_ratio`;
5. median of residual history available no later than `t-1`, transformed by
   `exp(-median)`;
6. 95th percentile of that prior residual history, transformed by
   `exp(-p95)`.

Current-frame residual, detector confidence, `base_quality`, source one-hot,
model identity, blend/bucket identifiers, and backend quality are excluded.
KLT-base, learned-seeded, and frozen classical-seeded rows use the same pooled
model. Source and geometry strata are audit groups only.

Let the fitted model output `p_hat_(i,t)`. On the calibration split compute
`s=|Y-p_hat|` and the finite-sample conformal quantile `Q_(1-alpha)` with
`alpha=0.10`. The selector receives exactly:

    q_lower_(i,t) = clip(p_hat_(i,t) - Q_(1-alpha), 0, 1).

There is no backend `min_quality` floor in this path. Backend q is separately
forced to one for controlled external-feature arms.

## 5. F/H Eligibility and Residual Normalization

Only sorted K0 correspondences are used to fit models. OpenCV RNG is reset to
`20260730` before each fit; confidence is `0.999`, maximum iterations `2000`,
and input order is ID ascending. F uses a 2.5 px threshold and at least 8
inliers; H uses a 5.0 px threshold and at least 4 inliers. No candidate is
added to a fit set and no arm may alter the fit.

For each candidate and each valid model `m`:

    e_m = clip(r_m / T_m, 0, e_max),   e_max = 4.0
    e_i = min over valid m of e_m

`r_F` is the one-sided pixel epipolar distance and `r_H` is the forward
homography reprojection distance. Both are dimensionless after division by
their frozen thresholds. A candidate passes when `e_i <= 1`. If no valid base
model exists, all new lineages are rejected with reason
`NO_VALID_BASE_MODEL`; there is no weak-model all-candidate fallback.

The selector uses `w_i = q_lower_i * exp(-e_i/tau_e)` with dimensionless
`tau_e=1.0`. Historical residuals enter q_lower only through the `t-1` feature
history; current e appears exactly once in the exponential.

## 6. K0/L/E, Budget, and Export

At a decision event:

- `K_t^0` is the immutable base producer set;
- `L_t` is the arm's already-admitted active set before this event;
- `E_t` is the arm-filtered subset of current eligible learned candidates
  that has never been admitted by that arm.

The concurrent accepted-lineage cap is `B_active=8`. New slots are
`b_t=max(0, B_active-|L_t|)`. The separate total exported-feature cap is 350;
the deterministic KLT base export reserves 342 slots, leaving at most 8
sidecar slots. The total cap is checked after combining base and active
lineages, not as a learned-lineage ceiling. H and P use the same caps.

P greedily admits the largest marginal gain until slots, candidate exhaustion,
or `min_gain=0.001`. H uses the frozen empty-4x6-cell, nearest-K0-distance,
canonical-ID ranking. Q ranks descending q_lower; G sets q_lower=1 and uses
the same marginal gain; R uses fixed-seed 3x3 grid-balanced pseudorandom
round-robin. Stable ties use ascending canonical ID.

The primary experiment uses the same master stream and the same maximum budget
for H and P. Because P may stop at `min_gain`, actual exported dose is a
reported outcome and is not forced equal. Development selector ablation solves
the dose/min_gain tension differently: at each common event, let
`n_t=|S_QG,t|`; R/H/Q/G take the first `n_t` entries of their complete
rankings. Subsequent lifetime and exported observations remain outcomes.

## 7. C-QG Matching Boundary

C-QG is generated from an independent classical pool at the learned trigger
frames. It executes the full pooled calibration and QG selector before any
matching. Matching is offline and outcome-blind with frozen trigger frame,
activation (within one frame), grid/spatial caliper, lifetime/observation
calipers, total-dose/peak-budget difference <=5%, continuous SMD <=0.10,
unmatched lineage <=20%, and unmatched observation dose <=10%. All learned
lineages remain in the balance denominator. The control is described as
matched-dose attribution, never as unconditional causality.

## 8. Shadow Promotion

P03A uses six fixed development windows already named in the companion plan.
It writes full R/H/Q/G/QG ranks, q/e/gain/reasons, master and frame-chain
hashes, per-event matched dose, H-QG overlap, and calibration/kernel/combined
latency. Four low/boundary windows and two normal windows are not changed after
the first shadow run.

`PROMOTE` requires synthetic contracts and calibration subgroup audits, QG
single-factor gates against both Q and G, positive selected-dose retention
>=90%, negative rank improvement >=0.10, and either overlap <0.90 or rank
margin improvement >=0.10. `BASELINE_ROUTE` requires contracts/audit but
overlap >=0.90 and rank increment <0.10; it maps P to the legacy H profile.
All other combinations are `REVISE`. A selector runtime P95 above 1 ms per
invoked frame is a P03B migration blocker and is reported, not re-labeled as
real-time.

## 9. Replay, Failure, and Statistics Contract

Each window x arm reserves three serial algorithmic VINS replays. A replay is
hard-failed by nonzero exit, timeout, empty/nonfinite/nonmonotonic trajectory,
initialization failure in the first 10 s, or full-window coverage below 50%.
Infrastructure failures retain the original run and are replaced before the
three algorithmic slots are reduced. A window-arm is hard-failed when fewer
than 2/3 algorithmic replays are evaluable; preserve 0/3, 1/3, 2/3, 3/3 and
`any_repeat_hard_failure`. Numeric metrics use the median of evaluable replays.
Solver risk is a separate any-of-3 binary event for linear-solver failure,
estimator reset/restart, or numerical abnormality signatures.

Primary trajectory unit is the independent sequence. For each window,
`r_w=log(APE_P/APE_comparator)`; sequence effects are equal-weight medians.
The fixed seed `20260730` drives a 10,000-draw hierarchical sequence/window
bootstrap and an exact two-sided sequence sign test. Report paired relative
improvement, IQR, CI, win count, failure denominator, coverage, solver risk,
learned observations, visual-factor cost, and runtime. No claim may be based
only on evaluable windows after dropping failures.

## 10. Current P03 Candidate Evidence and Blockers

Implemented and machine-tested candidate assets include:

- `master_candidate_stream.py` and deterministic F/H residual modules;
- independent GFTT proposer and source-neutral temporal calibrator;
- failure taxonomy and standard-library classifier;
- 40-frame classical A06 smoke and 20-frame XFeat A06 smoke with three source
  calibration rows and validated master-chain hashes;
- P03A shadow replay entry and synthetic selector tests.

The following remain explicitly unresolved before G2/P03 PASS:

1. canonical VINS exporter integration is not complete; current historical
   exporter output cannot be used to infer a selector-independent stream;
2. pooled calibrator has only smoke/development rows and is not frozen until
   the preregistered multi-sequence calibration split and subgroup gates pass;
3. current CPU-only PyTorch environment gives a development shadow selection
   P95 of approximately 282 ms in the A06 smoke, above the P03B 1 ms gate;
4. H/P canonical bag export, D whole-lineage derivation, and C-QG matching
   are not authorized yet;
5. no held-out or confirmatory learned/VINS result is claimed in P03.

These blockers keep stage P03 `IN_PROGRESS`; they are not failures of P02/G1.
